**Name:** OpenStream
**Short description (80):** A clean TV player for your own Stalker/MAG IPTV portal. No channels included.

**Long description**
OpenStream is an open-source player for Stalker and Ministra IPTV portals, built for the remote and the big screen.

- Live TV, TV Guide, Movies and Shows from your own subscription, with fast browsing of very large catalogs
- Search with suggestions, filters (language, genre, year, rating) and sorting
- Continue watching, favorites, next-episode and resume
- Profiles with a household PIN; Kids profiles use age labels, word filters and an allow-list of categories
- Optional online tags (your own free TMDB key) add genres and age certifications; optional IMDb ratings file for personal use
- Your portal address and MAC stay on your device, sealed by Android. No account, no ads, no tracking

OpenStream does not include or sell any content. You need your own subscription from a provider that supports MAG/Stalker
access with a registered MAC address. Use it only with content you are entitled to watch.
