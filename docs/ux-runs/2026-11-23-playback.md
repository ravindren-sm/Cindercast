## Fixes made during the run

1. **Next episode went backwards.** This portal lists seasons and episodes newest first (Season 2 above Season 1, E12 above
   E11), and the next-episode logic followed list order. It now orders seasons and episodes by the number in their names
   ("Season 2", "S02", "E11", "Episode 4") when every entry has one, else keeps the portal's order
   (`NextEpisodeLogic.inNumberOrder`, tests in `NextEpisodeTest`).
2. **Progress saved under the wrong episode.** The VOD player's listener and save-on-exit code kept the first episode's
   values; after an automatic next episode, Back saved the new episode's position and length under the old episode's name
   (Home showed "S02 E11 · 25 min left" for an episode watched to the end), and the old episode's resume point would have
   been applied to the new one. They now read the title playing now (`rememberUpdatedState`). Checked on the TV: after
   E11 ended and E12 played for 40 s, Home showed "S02 E12 · 24 min left" and E11 had left Continue watching.
