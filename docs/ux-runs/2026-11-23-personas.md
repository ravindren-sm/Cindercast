## Findings (numbered after the playback run's 26–32)

Major:
- **33. Up from the search box lands on "Clear filters".** It sits directly under the filter buttons, so a viewer pressing Up to
  reach the tabs passes through it, and one stray Select wipes the active filters (it happened in this run and in the afternoon
  run; "Use default" restores them). Fix: move "Clear filters" to the end of the filter row, or make Up from the search box go
  to the first filter button.
- **34. Fast scrolling in the poster grid drops presses and changes column.** In KIDS MOVIES, 20 quick Downs from the first
  poster moved about 5 rows and ended in the second column (poster 26, expected 100); 10 quick Downs from poster 41 ended on 65
  (expected 91) in the first column. Slow single Downs also jumped from the second column to the first once. The Live TV
  channel list does not have this problem.

Minor:
- **35.** The Live TV door on Home opens Live TV with focus on the tab bar, not in the page: one extra Down before anything can
  be chosen.
- **36.** Live TV opens on "All" after a restart, not the group used last; KIDS is the ninth group. Remembering the last group
  (or a Favorite channels row, which Home already offers once a channel is favourited) would cut 14 presses to about 3.
- **37.** Back from a tab shows Home but leaves focus on the old tab (TV Guide, Settings): the highlighted tab and the page
  shown disagree until the viewer moves.
- **38.** Search matches inside people's names: "lego" found Apollo 18 (director López-Gallego) and The Hanged Woman ("Found in:
  Cast") next to the kids' films. Match whole words in cast and director names.
- **39.** The suggestions shown while typing "lego" (five) did not include The Lego Movie; only the full results did.
- **40.** "Best rated" puts three obscure titles with Kinopoisk 10.0 above Bluey (IMDb 9.2): two rating scales mixed, and no
  allowance for how few people rated a title.
- **41.** A set Genre button reads "Animation", not "Genre: Animation" (Sort keeps its "Sort:" prefix).
- **42.** Settings: closing the rating picker with Back sends focus to the Settings tab, not to the row that opened it. (The
  profile switcher gets this right.)
- **43.** Settings: info-only rows ("Defaults applied", "What is sent", "What is downloaded", "Credit") are focus stops where
  Select does nothing.
- **44.** The rating picker says it "uses the portal's rating: IMDb when it has one, otherwise Kinopoisk"; scores from the IMDb
  file and the online tags (TMDB) are used too now.
- **45.** "Use my search defaults everywhere · Off" is followed by "Defaults applied · English · Fantasy · rating 7+", which reads
  as if they apply while the switch is off. Hide the row when Off, or call it "Defaults to apply".

Notes:
- **46.** Sports lists "SEC Storied" (college football) and "Boomer and Gio" (general sports talk) as NFL shows.

## Settings, reviewed as a critic

**Clear and safe**
- Grouped under plain headings (Profile, Portal, Content filter · this profile, Your viewing · this profile, Default search
  filters, Recent searches, Watching, Online tags (optional), IMDb ratings (personal use, optional), Memory, About). Each row
  states its current value and the action ("Shown · Hide", "On · Turn off").
- Every destructive action asks first, with Cancel: Reset (Most watched), Delete (Title index data), Clear (default filters,
  recent searches, Continue watching, favorites), Remove (IMDb ratings, key and tags). Only "Poster cache · Clear" does not,
  which is harmless.
- Privacy is explained where data leaves the device: "What is sent · Title names and years, with your key. Nothing else."; "What
  is downloaded · IMDb's free ratings file (about 8 MB)…"; About says portal details, favorites and history stay on the device.
- The IMDb credit uses IMDb's required wording.

**Works smoothly**
- Toggles ("Titles with no rating", "Play the next episode automatically") change at once, keep focus on the row, and were set
  back with a second press.
- The profile switcher ("Who's watching?") opens and closes cleanly.

**Could be better**
- Jargon a non-technical viewer will not know: "Title index", "Checking titles", "Tag service key", "Online tags". "Title index"
  and "Title index data · Delete" sit side by side with no line saying what the index is for (fast search and sorting).
- The technical groups (title index, online tags, IMDb file) sit between "Your viewing" and "Watching"; grouping them under an
  "Advanced" heading at the end would keep everyday settings together.
- Findings 42–45 above.
- Nothing for playback preferences (preferred audio language, subtitles on by default), which the Language and Subtitles drawer
  could remember.
