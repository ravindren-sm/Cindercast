# Saved searches (2026-11-24)

New "Saved" tab (adult profiles; hidden on Kids): named searches over the on-device title index, no typing, no portal request.
Each holds: look in (movies / shows / both), genres (up to 4, any-of or all-of), languages (the title's original language from
the online tags, up to 4), a minimum rating (5–8), a release year, and an order. Stored per profile; editable and deletable.

Checked on the TV (Family profile, titles listed by name only, nothing opened or played):
- Created "Comedy / Sci-Fi, English, rating 6+" through the form: any-of gave 7,823 titles (first 600 shown); all-of
  ("Comedy + Sci-Fi") gave 346, led by Rick and Morty, Maniac, Smiling Friends: a real sci-fi comedy list.
- The search survived an app restart; Edit reopens it with its values; changing any-of to all-of and saving re-ran it.
- Found and fixed during the run: Up from the first grid row did not reach Edit (it went to the list); Down from the last form
  row landed on "Delete this search" (now Save); a saved auto-name did not follow later edits (now it does, unless renamed).
- Known limits: language is the original language per the tags (so a Tamil dub of an English series matches "English");
  titles with no tags (about 24,000 of 104,571) cannot match genre or language; results show the first 600.
